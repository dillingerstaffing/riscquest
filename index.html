      The DM's Notes

      how to play
      • The loop: the scout pins bounties every 12 hours, I draft the battle plan, you fight the duel and report back in chat. The Combat Manual above is the complete mapping.
      • Seasons run on calendar months. Road to the Throne dates recalibrate monthly from your actuals.
      • Nothing is ever erased. Completed ranks, milestones, seals, quests, and seasons keep their dates forever. The Chronicle below is the full saga, newest first.
      This game has no grind. Glory flows from battle plans, accepts, and upvotes, weighted toward real outcomes. It never flows from post count: spamming weak answers earns nothing and gets you exiled (banned). The game respects you; play it like it matters, because the throne is real.
